The fictional project brief

A four-week onboarding pilot ran in six stores with 120 new hires. Median certification time went from 18 to 12 days. Day-30 readiness moved from 6.5 to 8.1 out of 10. There was no control group. Leaders need to decide whether to extend the pilot. Do not claim the pilot caused the changes or invent cost savings.
